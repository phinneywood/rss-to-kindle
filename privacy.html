Privacy

Morning Reader stores your sign-in email, Send-to-Kindle address, feeds, delivery settings, and delivery metadata to provide the service.

Article content is fetched to build EPUB files and is not intended to be kept as a permanent article archive. Authentication codes and sessions expire.

Morning Reader uses Supabase for application data, Resend for email delivery, Vercel for the web interface, and Amazon Send to Kindle as the destination you configure.

This is a beta policy and will be expanded before broader release.
